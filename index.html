Recommendation

Use AI to accelerate understanding—not to replace evidence.

Rules first

Use deterministic checks for facts and business constraints.

Contract enforced

Require typed JSON and controlled error labels.

Human controlled

Route missing, conflicting, or invalid output to review.

The trusted result is the workflow—not a single AI score.
